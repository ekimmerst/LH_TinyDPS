<h1 style="color: #d9a94b;">LH TinyDPS</h1>

<p>View damage and healing from WoW's built-in meter data in a compact, customizable window with fight and overall totals.</p>

<p>A small damage and healing window for players who want a quick read without a second full-size meter. LH TinyDPS uses WoW's built-in damage meter data in its own compact display; it does not change Blizzard's meter window.</p>

<h2 style="color: #73c9c4;">What you can see</h2>

<ul>
  <li>Damage or healing for the current fight, the overall session or earlier fights available in the game.</li>
  <li>Ranks, totals, DPS or HPS and group share, with options to keep just the numbers you prefer.</li>
  <li>Row tooltips with more detail, plus chat reporting when you want to share a result.</li>
</ul>

<h2 style="color: #c78be6;">Make it yours</h2>

<p>Open <strong>Options → AddOns → LH Tweaks → LH TinyDPS</strong> to move and resize the window, change bars, colors and text, or choose when it is visible. Its minimap button can toggle the window and open settings. You can also type <code>/lh_tinydps help</code> for commands. If LH Stats is installed, the default layout sits beneath it. The default width matches LH ExpBar when installed; Reset layout applies its current width without changing a custom width until you reset.</p>

<h2 style="color: #c78be6;">Settings profiles</h2>

<p>Choose a character under <strong>Use settings from</strong> to share window position, size, colors, text, display mode, visibility, minimap button and other general options. LH Tweaks can apply its selected source to all LH addons. The selected combat session and the game's meter data remain individual. <strong>This character</strong> restores your own display settings.</p>

<h2 style="color: #73c9c4;">More LH addons</h2>

<p>Choose only the pieces you want. Most companion addons require LH Tweaks; LH NameplateQuestInfo also works without it.</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>LH AuraBars:</strong> Compact bars for buffs, debuffs and spell cooldowns.</li>
  <li><strong>LH CooldownPulse:</strong> Flashes an ability icon when its cooldown finishes.</li>
  <li><strong>LH ExpBar:</strong> A minimal experience bar with rested XP.</li>
  <li><strong>LH HideMe:</strong> Hides selected parts of the default UI, individually or all at once.</li>
  <li><strong>LH JobsDone:</strong> Plays the Warcraft peasant voice when a quest is ready to turn in.</li>
  <li><strong>LH MailBags:</strong> Opens your bags at the mailbox, with optional closing.</li>
  <li><strong>LH MP5:</strong> Shows an estimated five-second timer on the player mana bar.</li>
  <li><strong>LH NameplateQuestInfo:</strong> Adds quest markers and objective progress to nameplates.</li>
  <li><strong>LH NameplateThreatColors:</strong> Colors Blizzard nameplates by threat and role.</li>
  <li><strong>LH PetStats:</strong> Shows hunter pet loyalty beside the pet portrait.</li>
  <li><strong>LH Range:</strong> Shows melee range, configurable spell-range zones for every class, and a live non-Mana resource bar in melee range.</li>
  <li><strong>LH BuffReminders:</strong> Reminds you when selected class buffs are missing.</li>
  <li><strong>LH Skills:</strong> Shows profession ranks in a movable compact bar.</li>
  <li><strong>LH Stats:</strong> Shows money, free bag slots and remaining ammo below the minimap.</li>
  <li><strong>LH SwingTimer:</strong> Shows main-hand and ranged swing timers below the player cast bar.</li>
  <li><strong>LH Tweaks:</strong> Provides the shared settings hub plus chat, positioning, merchant and fishing tweaks.</li>
</ul>

<h2 style="color: #d9a94b;">Installation and feedback</h2>

<p><strong>LH Tweaks is required</strong> and must be installed alongside LH TinyDPS. LH Stats is optional. I built this display for my own UI, and I hope it is useful to others who like a smaller meter. Please leave feedback, problems and ideas in the CurseForge comments.</p>

<p>Thanks to Sideshow and Talyrius for <a href="https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/tinydps">TinyDPS</a> and the inspiration behind a compact meter. LH TinyDPS is a separate display built around WoW's own meter data.</p>
